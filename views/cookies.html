What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Local storage is similar browser storage used for preferences and recent activity.

What we use

  • ipscope-cookie-consent (local storage) — Records that you accepted the cookie notice. Essential for compliance display.
  • ipscope-theme (local storage) — Saves your light/dark theme preference.
  • ipscope-recent (local storage) — Stores your recent IP lookups on this device only.

Third-party cookies

We do not load web fonts from any third party — the site uses the fonts already installed on your device. The only third-party requests are map tiles from OpenStreetMap, and those are made solely when a map is displayed. OpenStreetMap may log connection data, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy. We set no advertising cookies and use no cross-site tracking.

If privacy-respecting analytics are enabled on this instance, they are cookie-free and collect no personally identifying information, so they set nothing in your browser to disclose here.

Managing cookies

You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings. Clearing storage will reset theme, recent lookups, and consent banner.
